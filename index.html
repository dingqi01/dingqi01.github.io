About

Qi Ding

Ph.D. candidate at the Institute of Energy, Environment, and Economy, Tsinghua University

I study how transport and energy systems can transition toward carbon neutrality, with a focus on electric mobility, power-system interactions, and low-carbon fuels.

My work combines integrated assessment, optimization, spatial analysis, and data-driven modeling. I aim to connect long-term transition pathways with the operational and infrastructure detail needed for credible policy decisions.
