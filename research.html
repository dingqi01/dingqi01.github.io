Research approach

I use models as structured ways to connect evidence across scales—from national transition pathways and provincial infrastructure to charging operations and technology choices. The emphasis is on explicit boundaries, traceable data, reproducible scenario comparisons, and conclusions that remain meaningful under uncertainty.
