Research

Systems questions at the intersection of mobility, energy, and climate.

My research examines transitions that cross conventional sector boundaries. The common objective is to connect long-term pathways with operational and spatial detail—so that decarbonization strategies remain credible when infrastructure, behavior, and uncertainty matter.

Methods

From pathways to operations

  • Integrated assessment and energy-system modeling
  • Mathematical optimization and scenario analysis
  • Techno-economic and lifecycle assessment
  • GIS, spatial analysis, and data-driven modeling
  • Power-system and transport-model coupling
  • Climate-stress and robustness analysis

Working principle

Models are most useful when their boundaries are explicit, their evidence is traceable, and their results can be connected to real infrastructure and policy decisions.
