# Design rationale

The redesign combines two complementary academic-site patterns:

- [Felix Schaumann](https://felixschaumann.github.io/): a compact profile column, readable biography, restrained typography, and conventional academic navigation.
- [Zhuang Liu](https://liuzhuang13.github.io/): high information density, concise research-topic summaries, alternating publication rows, and real paper figures.

The resulting site uses a 960px content width, Source Sans 3, a white and neutral-gray palette, and a single blue-green accent. The homepage starts with identity and research focus rather than a marketing headline. The Research page connects end-use transitions, spatial infrastructure and supply chains, and hourly power-system integration in one three-stage framework; ongoing work is labeled by status and separated from public results. Publications use compact rows and paper-specific figures; output types are separated so that peer-reviewed work, preprints, manuscripts, presentations, and reports cannot be confused.

The CV page uses the same restrained hierarchy for education, selected institutional projects, professional experience, and honors. A single two-page English academic CV is maintained in LaTeX and shown through two page previews; the site does not maintain competing short, full, Chinese, or English CV variants.

The site intentionally omits analytics, animation, dark mode, a blog, bilingual content, project-showcase cards, and unverified academic or social-profile links.
